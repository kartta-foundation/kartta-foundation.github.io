01 / SERVICES FOR COUNSEL

When location matters,
the details matter.

A persuasive map still needs a sound technical basis. We help legal teams examine the sources, assumptions, and processing steps behind geospatial evidence.

01

Examine geospatial data

Review GIS layers, attributes, metadata, and available source records. Identify inconsistencies, missing information, and questions about how a dataset was created or modified.

Focus: provenance & data quality

02

Reconstruct historical context

Extract and compare information from historical maps and geographic records. Assess alignment, map scale, and changes over time before drawing conclusions.

Focus: historical map analysis

03

Evaluate spatial claims

Examine the methods behind boundaries, distances, overlays, and counts. Assess whether different inputs or assumptions change the result.

Focus: methods & uncertainty

04

Explain the findings

Translate technical analysis into clear written findings and annotated maps. Help counsel identify follow-up questions and understand the limits of the available evidence.

Focus: clear technical communication

02 / OUR APPROACH

Follow the evidence.
Make the reasoning clear.

The scope starts with your technical question. The analysis should make its sources, methods, and limitations understandable to counsel.

  1. Define the question

    Discuss the matter at a high level, check for conflicts, and agree on the scope and handling of materials before substantive review.

  2. Trace and examine

    Identify the available sources and processing history. Compare datasets and test the assumptions relevant to the question.

  3. Document and explain

    Present the findings with supporting sources, methods, and limitations. Distinguish observations from interpretations and unresolved questions.
