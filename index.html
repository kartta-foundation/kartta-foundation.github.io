Our Tools

We develop machine learning tools that turn historical maps into usable geographic information for research and public understanding.

  github.com/kartta-foundation

mapKurator

Detects, extracts, and geocodes text from scanned historical maps.

  • Paper: The mapKurator System: A Complete Pipeline for Extracting and Linking Text from Historical Maps

PALETTE

Detects and recognizes text on historical maps, with support for English, Traditional Chinese, and Japanese.

  • Paper: Hyper-local deformable transformers for text spotting on historical maps

LIGHT

Groups map text into meaningful phrases using spatial relationships, visual cues, and language context.

  • Paper: LIGHT: Multi-modal Text Linking on Historical Maps

Geospatial Digital Forensics

Our geospatial digital forensics work extends Kartta Foundation’s nonprofit mission to distill and assemble geographic knowledge for the public good. We help attorneys and legal teams examine and understand complex geographic evidence.

We combine GIS expertise with custom software to make large evidence collections easier to analyze and review. Our analysis documents sources, methods, and uncertainty so findings can be evaluated carefully.

Custom Tools for Each Case

We rapidly build custom tools to collect, organize, and analyze large volumes of public data and digital evidence. Automating repetitive work helps reduce review time and shorten the overall project timeline.

Data integration, automated processing, and review tools tailored to the case.

Geospatial Evidence Analysis

We examine GIS datasets, imagery, historical maps, and location records. We compare sources, check spatial relationships and metadata, and identify discrepancies and uncertainty.

Source comparison, spatial analysis, and historical reconstruction.

Clear Findings & Expert Support

We turn technical analysis into reports, annotated maps, chronologies, and visualizations. We document sources, methods, and limitations, and support counsel with technical discovery and expert testimony.

Traceable findings that help legal teams understand the evidence.

We protect client confidentiality and share engagement details only with authorization.
